---
title: Constraints are editorial
date: 2026-07-21
summary: A short note on why fewer fields can produce a better record.
published: true
tags: [design, systems]
---

Every field in a tool quietly asks the user to pay attention to something.

A project log with fields for mood, duration, category, priority, confidence, and outcome does more than store information. It edits the project into those categories. That may be useful, but it is not neutral.

The best small tools often make a strong editorial decision about what deserves to be recorded, then leave the rest alone.
