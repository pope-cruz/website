---
title: Naming the next step
date: 2026-07-14
summary: Vague project states usually hide a missing decision.
published: true
tags: [process]
---

“Continue working on it” is not a useful next step.

The phrase usually means one of three things: the desired outcome is unclear, the next decision has not been made, or the task is still too large to picture. Naming a visible action reveals which problem is present.

This is why a build log should end with something concrete enough to begin without rereading the entire log.
