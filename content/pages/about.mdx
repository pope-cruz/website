hi! i'm pope.

i'm a computer science student at nyu from the philippines. i enjoy building small software, documenting ideas, and occasionally overengineering personal projects.

i'm also interested in the people side of technology: organizing events, bringing people together, helping projects find an audience, and making technical spaces easier to participate in.

most of what i build starts with a problem that's annoying enough for me to solve for myself. if it turns out useful, i keep working on it. if not, at least i learned something.

this website is where i keep track of what i'm building, thinking about, and helping bring to life.

## a few things about me

- currently studying computer science at nyu
- management & public policy minor
- interested in software, startups, education, and technology policy
- especially interested in community, product, and go-to-market
- usually switching between new york and manila
- i write things down because i forget otherwise

## community and organizing

i like creating spaces where people can meet, learn from each other, and work on things together.

at nyu, that has meant helping organize technical events and thinking about how communities, companies, and student builders can collaborate. i enjoy introducing people, shaping ideas into something others can understand, and getting people excited enough to participate.
