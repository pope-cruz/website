## Why it exists

Daily notes are too granular for reviewing a project, while monthly reviews arrive too late to steer it. Weeklog sits between them. It asks for a small record of decisions, progress, and unresolved questions once a week.

## Current shape

Each entry is a plain text file with four prompts:

1. What changed?
2. What did I learn?
3. What is blocked?
4. What happens next?

The format is intentionally boring. The experiment is about whether a reliable review rhythm improves the work, not whether the logging interface can become a product.

## Build log

### 2026-07-22

Moved entries to Markdown and removed a custom editor. A regular file is easier to search, diff, and keep after the application changes.

### 2026-07-15

Reduced the template from seven prompts to four. The longer version encouraged complete sentences when a short fact would have been more useful.
