Steppi was a short mobile prototype built around a single constraint: every project should expose one next action.

## What changed

The first version allowed nested plans. That recreated the planning system I was trying to avoid. The final prototype kept only three pieces of information:

- the project name
- the next action
- the date it was last changed

## Why it is archived

The constraint was useful, but it worked just as well in an existing notes app. Keeping a separate application did not improve the behavior enough to justify its maintenance.

Archiving it was the successful outcome. The prototype answered the question it was built to answer.
